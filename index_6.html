Note
Demo only. Heart rate is derived from real optical PPG. Blood pressure uses HRV-derived heuristics and is not medically accurate. Do not use for clinical decisions.
Heart rate is measured using optical photoplethysmography — the camera's red channel detects subtle colour changes in your fingertip as blood pulses through capillaries. HRV is derived from the interval between detected peaks. Blood pressure is estimated via HRV heuristics, not a pressure cuff. All processing happens on-device in real time.
